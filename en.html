Your place to
make a difference.

Discovering that you have something to give, that someone needs you and that you can do something for another person is one of the best feelings there is. There is nothing like being the right person at the right time. It gives us meaning, belonging and a sense of fulfillment.

But how do you find your place to make a difference? There are plenty of questions and obstacles along the way: What do I have to offer? Where am I needed? How do I fit it into an already busy life?

That is where I come in: to work out, together, how to turn the wish to contribute into action that brings meaning and belonging. I’m Maayan, a social activist, a reservist and a mother. I’m here to help you find your place to make a difference.

Your place to make a difference

These sessions are for people who want to do something for others but find it hard to know where to begin or how to move forward.

What will we do together?

We’ll explore what you have to offer, what matters to you and what is holding you back. We’ll look at how your experience, skills and hopes could meet someone else’s need.

What will you take away?

A clearer direction and a practical plan that fits your time, energy and life.

Ask about 1:1 consulting

Questions about 1:1 consulting

+
How do the sessions work?
This is a series of individual sessions, on Zoom or in person in Tel Aviv. Message me on WhatsApp to discuss the number and length of sessions and the fee.
Can I come without an idea or previous experience?
Yes. We’ll start with your wish to contribute, the experience you already have and what you can offer. You don’t need to arrive with a fully formed project.
What if I’m already involved and want to move forward?
We’ll look at what is working, where you could make a difference and which steps could help you move forward, whether in a project, volunteer work or your job.
How can I fit this into everyday life?
We’ll take your time and energy into account. Contributing doesn’t always mean adding something to your schedule: there may be opportunities within the work, family or community already in your life.

Taking action for others

The courage to reach out

+

We see someone who needs help or think of something worth doing, and then we hesitate. Perhaps it isn’t our role. Perhaps we don’t have enough time. Perhaps someone else is already taking care of it. Through stories from my own work, we’ll talk about that hesitation and what helps us move past it. You’ll leave with ideas for recognizing what you have to offer and where you are needed, and more courage to take a first step toward action that brings belonging, meaning and energy to keep going.

From writing to action

From posts to projects

+

How does a social media post turn into something that happens in the real world? Through stories from my own work, we’ll explore writing that connects a need with a response, bringing people and resources together, and how social media can lead to action for others.

+

Three ways of taking action, inspired by women in Jewish texts. Through learning, conversation and writing, we’ll explore different ways of responding, what these women can teach us and how their stories connect with our choices and actions today.

Questions about talks and workshops

+
Who are the talks for, and can you tailor one to our audience?
The talks and workshops are for organizations, education teams, communities and leadership programs. We’ll speak beforehand about your audience and what matters to you, then adapt the topic and focus. No previous experience in social action is needed.
What will people take away?
The aim is to encourage people to take part and to trust their ability to make a difference. Through stories and real-life questions, we’ll consider what each of us has to offer, what holds us back and where we can begin.
What is the difference between a talk and a workshop, and how long is each?
A talk centers on stories and ideas. A workshop makes more room for participation, conversation and individual or shared work. We’ll agree on the length in advance, according to the format, audience and time available.
How do we book a talk or workshop, and what does it cost?
Message me on WhatsApp with a little about your audience, event and planned date. We’ll choose a suitable talk or workshop together. The fee is agreed with me, based on the event, session length and any adaptations needed.
A place to act together

Women’s peer group

Four mastermind sessions for thinking things through together, learning from one another and finding your direction for action.

  • 4 sessions
  • 2 hours per session
  • Up to 10 women

It can be easier together: sharing ideas, getting feedback from peers and being part of a group.

We’ll explore what matters to each participant, what she has to offer and where she wants to contribute in a way that brings belonging and meaning.

We’ll work with real situations and questions brought by participants. Through shared experience and questions, each woman will clarify her direction and plan steps that fit her life.

For upcoming dates, location and fees, get in touch on WhatsApp.

Ask about the group

Questions about the group

+
Who is the group for?
Women who are still looking for a direction, as well as those already involved who want to focus or consider a change.
Do I need to come with an idea?
No. You can bring a question, a dilemma or a wish that hasn’t taken shape yet.
How is the group different from 1:1 consulting?
In individual sessions, the time is devoted to your own process. In the group, you work on your questions while also learning from the situations, experience and perspectives of other participants.
What can I learn from someone else’s situation?
Their situation may help you recognize a familiar obstacle, discover a new possibility or understand more clearly what is right for you.
What will I take away from the process?
The aim is a clearer direction and steps you can put into practice: moving an idea forward, changing something you already do or choosing where to begin.

Nice to meet you

Through writing and initiative, I bring people together to act. I bring this experience to my individual consulting, talks and workshops.

I’m a mother of five daughters, married to Elishav and an IDF reservist. I believe we can make things better.
