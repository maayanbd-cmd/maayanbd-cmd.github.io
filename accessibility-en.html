Accessibility statement

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Maayan Rabinovich is committed to making information about her consulting, talks, workshops and peer groups available to people with disabilities, and to addressing accessibility barriers.

Accessibility work on this website

The accessibility work is guided by Israeli Standard SI 5568 Part 1, level AA, and the applicable Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Accessibility Adjustments to Services) Regulations, 2013. Full conformance has not yet been verified.

  • A skip link, headings, navigation landmarks and alternative text for informative images.
  • Keyboard operation for navigation, expandable questions and the review carousel, with visible focus indicators.
  • Readable text contrast, responsive layouts and browser zoom support without disabling magnification.
  • Testimonials start paused. Automatic rotation can be started and paused with a labelled button; reduced-motion preferences are respected.
  • Labelled blog search, filter buttons and status messages.

How to use the website

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter to follow a link, and Enter or Space to operate a button or expandable question. Escape closes the mobile menu. When the reviews region is focused, use the arrow keys to move between reviews and Home/End to reach the first/last position. Use your browser’s zoom controls to enlarge the page.

Checks and known limitations

The HTML, styles and interaction code have been reviewed. Browser checks cover Chrome, keyboard interaction and narrow screens. These checks do not constitute a full accessibility audit. Manual testing with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, and independent verification against all applicable criteria, have not yet been completed.

The blog and its articles are in Hebrew. WhatsApp and Facebook links open external services in a new tab. Their accessibility is controlled by those services. If an external service is difficult to use, you can contact Maayan by telephone.

Accessibility of meetings and events

The venue for in-person meetings has not yet been selected. Accessibility arrangements will be updated here once the venue is chosen. In the meantime, contact Maayan about any accessibility adjustments you need for a meeting, talk or workshop, or to ask about online consulting.

Contact about accessibility

Contact: Maayan Rabinovich. You can send a WhatsApp message about accessibility.

Phone

If you encounter a barrier or need the information in another accessible format, please share the page address, what you were trying to do, a description of the difficulty and how to contact you. Browser and assistive-technology details are helpful if available. Maayan will review the request and coordinate an appropriate way to provide the information or service.
